# Start here

Read both companion documents before acting:

1. `claude_ca_story_and_research_handoff.md` — how the idea developed, the user's corrections, reference papers/repositories/data, and room to explore. It contains an annotated conversation record, not a full verbatim transcript.
2. `claude_ca_build_prompt.md` — operational research and build brief.

Start with source inspection and small feasibility experiments, then build a working vertical slice. Treat our proposed methods as hypotheses: challenge them and explore alternatives. Don't stop at a research report, and don't spend the first session polishing the UI. First establish whether you can recover known CA rules, synthesize a short animation, and independently replay an exported discrete circuit. Measure pilot runtime before scheduling larger experiments. Keep the full product and larperCord design direction in view.

First checkpoint: a real tiny search result, measured limitations, and a justified next experiment.

Proceed with the authorized local research and implementation. No public deployment. Preserve existing work and follow repository instructions. Read the story for intent and the build brief for execution; if an implementation suggestion is contradicted by evidence, explain the evidence and adapt. Do not silently weaken the compact-rule or autonomous-playback objectives to obtain an attractive demo.
